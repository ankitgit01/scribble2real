# Mentor guide

## Dashboard at a glance

Open `/mentor` on the host laptop or a mentor tablet and enter the PIN. The overview shows the queue, active generation, average image time, GPU memory, and explanation usage. Activity cards show recent prompts and results; the Devices list lets you block or unblock a browser.

## Controls

- **Pause / Resume** stops or allows new image requests. Add a short banner to give everyone the same prompt or update.
- **Explanations enabled** turns the Learn panel on or off. Generation still works when it is off.
- **Active jobs per student** sets how many images one browser can have in progress.
- **Clear waiting jobs** removes queued work without interrupting the image currently running.
- **Show join QR** displays the public or local link for students to scan. Choose another address if the first one is not reachable from their network.
- **Block** stops a device from submitting new requests. Use **Unblock** to restore access.

## A simple session flow

1. Start with a two-minute warm-up: ask students to sketch an object using only a few shapes.
2. Share a class prompt in the banner, such as “Imagine a tiny greenhouse on Mars”.
3. Invite pairs to take turns sketching and trying one image style. Ask them to compare the output with their lines and adjust one detail.
4. Let students explore the explanation panel when enabled. Remind them that AI explanations can be wrong and important facts should be checked.
5. End with a short reflection: which lines helped the image follow the sketch, and what would they change next time?

Keep prompts short, watch the queue, and switch to pairs or rounds when several students are waiting. For connection, quota, or GPU problems, use the [runbook](RUNBOOK.md).
