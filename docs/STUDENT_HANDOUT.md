# Make a sketch come alive

1. Draw a few clear shapes on the canvas, or add a photo of your paper sketch.
2. Add a short image idea if you like. Pick a style, then tap **Make it**.
3. Compare your lines with the image. Download it, try a variation, or sketch over it.
4. Ask a science or design question in your sketch to explore it in the Learn panel.

## Sketch tips

- Draw the main outline large enough to see.
- Leave a little space between separate objects.
- Add a few details that make your idea recognizable.
- If you take a photo, keep the page flat and use bright, even light.

## A few things to know

Your mentors can see what you draw and type. Please do not enter personal details such as your name, phone number, or school. If explanations are enabled, an online AI service helps write them; double-check important facts with a trusted source.

Magic is a creative helper. It may miss details, change parts of your sketch, or make mistakes. Try another prompt or draw a clearer outline if the result is not what you imagined.
